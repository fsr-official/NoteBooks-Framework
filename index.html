NoteBooks is where notes stop being static. Diagrams that show how ideas connect, callouts that catch what matters before you miss it, and a structure built to survive the second read — not just the first. Study a concept once, and the page is still useful the tenth time you come back to it.

Why NoteBooksLearning that compounds

Built for understanding. Notes are organized for study, revision, and returning to difficult ideas without losing context.

Improved in public. Contributions move through validation, review, and the project’s GitHub workflow before they become part of the shared shelf.

Connected by conversation. Community discussions and Issues turn questions, gaps, and corrections into visible next steps.
